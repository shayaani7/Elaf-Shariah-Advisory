Project Highlights

A selection of high-impact engagements that demonstrate the breadth and depth of our advisory capabilities.

Banking

Shariah-Compliant Credit Card

Designed a fully compliant, regulator-ready Shariah credit card framework for a prominent U.S. banking institution. The engagement included product structuring, fee architecture design, and comprehensive Shariah documentation to satisfy both federal regulators and the bank's internal compliance board.

Telecom

Islamic Insurance (Takaful) Model

Structured a comprehensive Islamic insurance (Takaful) model for a multinational telecom provider. The model addressed risk-sharing mechanisms, Wakala fee structures, and participant surplus distribution — all engineered for seamless integration into the provider's existing corporate insurance framework.

FinTech

Digital Murabaha Calculator

Engineered a digital Murabaha calculator for a global fintech platform. The tool automates cost-plus financing calculations with built-in Shariah compliance checks, supporting multiple currencies and regulatory jurisdictions for a seamless user experience.

Social Finance

Islamic Microfinance Ecosystem

Structured a high-impact Islamic microfinance ecosystem designed for poverty alleviation. The framework includes Qard Hasan (benevolent lending), Murabaha micro-financing, and Zakat distribution channels — creating a sustainable social impact model aligned with Maqasid al-Shariah.

Digital Assets

Halal Investment Strategies

Reviewed digital asset frameworks, token projects, and structured halal investment strategies using U.S. index funds. Delivered definitive compliance rulings on specific cryptocurrency ecosystems and guided institutional investors on building Shariah-compliant digital portfolios.
